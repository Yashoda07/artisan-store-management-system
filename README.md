# Artisan Store Management System – Odoo

An Odoo-based ERP project designed to simulate the end-to-end operations of an artisan retail business, including product management, inventory tracking, customer management, sales orders, and invoicing.

## Project Overview

The Artisan Store Management System demonstrates how an ERP platform can integrate different business operations into a single workflow.

The project uses Odoo's standard business applications to simulate the operations of an artisan marketplace, where customers can purchase handcrafted products and the business can manage products, inventory, sales, and invoices.

## Objectives

- Understand the fundamentals of ERP systems and business process management.
- Configure an Odoo environment for a retail business workflow.
- Manage artisan products and product categories.
- Track product inventory.
- Manage customer information.
- Create and process sales orders.
- Generate invoices from sales transactions.
- Understand how different business processes are connected within an ERP system.

## Business Scenario

The system represents a small artisan retail business selling handcrafted products such as:

- Warli Paintings
- Handmade Bags
- Terracotta Pots
- Hand-painted Vases
- Wooden Crafts

The workflow simulates a customer purchasing an artisan product and the business processing the transaction through Odoo.

## Modules Used

### Inventory
Used to:

- Create and categorize products
- Maintain product information
- Track available stock
- Monitor inventory levels

### Sales
Used to:

- Create customer records
- Create quotations
- Create sales orders
- Process customer purchases

### Invoicing
Used to:

- Generate invoices from sales orders
- Maintain transaction records
- Track invoice status

## End-to-End Workflow

```text
Product Creation
       ↓
Inventory Configuration
       ↓
Customer Creation
       ↓
Quotation
       ↓
Sales Order
       ↓
Invoice Generation
```
